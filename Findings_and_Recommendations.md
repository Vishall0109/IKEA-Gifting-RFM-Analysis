# Findings and recommendations

Author: Vishal Solanke. Based on 5,881 identified customers and ~17.37M in revenue (Dec 2009 to Dec 2011). Churned means no purchase in the 90 days before 10 Dec 2011.

## Findings

1. Revenue is highly concentrated. Champions are 22.0% of customers but 68.1% of revenue, with an average value of about 9,169 per customer, roughly five times Loyal or At Risk customers.
2. Half the base has churned: 50.86% of customers made no purchase in the last 90 days.
3. At Risk customers are worth saving. There are 821 of them, they average about 1,932 each (similar to Loyal), account for 9.1% of revenue, and all of them are already past the 90-day mark.
4. Lost customers are numerous but low value: 1,524 customers (25.9%) bring in 3.8% of revenue, about 429 each.
5. New customers (442) have bought recently but only once or twice; they are the pool the next Loyal customers come from.
6. Revenue peaks in Q4, which fits a gifting business.

## Recommended actions by segment

| Segment | Customers | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champions | 1,291 | Early access, loyalty perks, personal outreach. Do not discount. | They already buy; protecting them protects about two thirds of revenue. |
| Loyal | 1,416 | Cross-sell and bundle offers, a nudge towards Champion-level spend. | Steady buyers; 31.5% have started to lapse. |
| At Risk | 821 | Win-back campaign with a time-limited offer, ahead of Q4. | High past value, all churned, best return per contact. |
| New | 442 | Welcome series and a second-purchase incentive. | Turning one-time buyers into repeat buyers is the cheapest growth. |
| Needs Attention | 387 | Reminder emails with products they bought before. | 51.7% already churned; low frequency means they need a reason to return. |
| Lost | 1,524 | One low-cost reactivation attempt, then stop. | Low value, so do not spend heavily. |

## Caveats
The 90-day cutoff is a simple rule, wholesale and retail buyers are not separated, and the data predates any promotions information, so these are directions to test, not guarantees.
